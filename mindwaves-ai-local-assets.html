Research Focus · Autonomous Bioproduction

AUTOBIO-KI

Scalable Evaluation and Robotics System for Automated High-Performance Biomass Production through AI Integration

Target Programme
BMBF — "Zukunft der Wertschöpfung"
Future of Value Creation
Role
Lead Research Partner

MindWaves is developing research capabilities for fully AI-controlled biological production — combining Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs), real-time Computer Vision, and autonomous Edge-AI robotics into a closed-loop "Sim-to-Real" architecture for industrial biomass cultivation.

MindWaves AI leads the development of the Edge-AI vision pipeline (YOLOv8 / DINOv2) and the autonomous control logic deployed directly on edge hardware (Raspberry Pi 5 + Hailo-8L NPU).

MindWaves Contribution

  • Edge-AI Computer Vision pipeline (biomass classification, anomaly detection)
  • Autonomous control logic for robotic sensor systems
  • Real-time inference at sub-100 ms latency on edge devices
  • Research coordination & technical leadership

Research Network

  • Energaia Labs GmbH — Industrial bioconversion & system validation
  • Gemino GmbH — Cloud infrastructure & data architecture
  • Epic Technologies UG — Mechatronic gantry systems & sensor integration
  • Robert Boyle Institut e.V. — PINNs, Digital Twin & scientific leadership
Key Innovation

The "Sim-to-Real Loop" — a closed feedback cycle where Physics-Informed Neural Networks continuously calibrate against real sensor data, enabling predictive resource optimisation that purely data-driven models cannot achieve.
